This reverses the two-writer contract. When a build's scope includes the review, a second
short wiki pass after the loop records the corrections the loop applied, on the run branch.
`/ss:correct`, on its branch, supersedes the correction fact it encoded with a fact naming
the check that now enforces the rule, and writes nothing else.
