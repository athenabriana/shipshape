A situational skill is guidance an agent loads mid-task when its situation matches a
trigger. It is created only when the trigger is observable ("when X happens"), at least two
skills or agents need it, and it is judgment: what runs the same way every time becomes a
script or a check. The test sits at the top of `references/triggers.md`.
