In `/ss:diagnose`, the reproduction of a performance bug is a benchmark run with
`explain-the-number`: five alternated runs, median and range, the limiter named. The verdict
is `faster`, `slower`, `no measurable difference` or `inconclusive`. When local runs are
forbidden the same protocol runs in CI.
