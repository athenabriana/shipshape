# bug-fix

Take a bug from a short report (a pasted error, a stack trace, "fix this bug") to a proven fix, with no spec.

## Steps

1. Run `bug-fix`: `/ss:bug-fix` holds the steps, from the cause to the shipped fix, and copies them into the todo list.

## Route

The skill's own `## Route`: sized with `<plugin-root>/scripts/route.py` at its step 5, with the reproduction and the focused test as its goals on the standard and intensive routes.

## Endpoint

The skill's own `## Endpoint`: local commit, pushed branch or open PR, asked once when the request leaves it open.
